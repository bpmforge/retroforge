# RetroForge v0.10.0

Commit `6736dbba58208b7627df9338afd8588d3abacb2a`

## What's new in 0.10.0

Three weeks of work since 0.9.1. Everything below is off until you turn it
on, and Original mode still shows exactly what the console drew.

- **Widescreen up to 32:9, with a level scout.** Widescreen now fills 16:9
  or 32:9 from what the game itself draws. A hidden second copy of the game
  runs a little ahead of you so scenery appears in the margins before you
  reach it, and remembered levels load instantly on the next visit. For a
  game without settings, **Find scout settings for this game** works them
  out from a minute of play.
- **Soft-patching.** `.bps`, `.ips` and `.ups` patches (including widescreen
  patches and the bsnes-hd `.bso` convention) are applied in memory, never
  to your ROM. Patched sessions are labelled and keep their own save states.
- **HD-2D look and Diorama.** One card stands a 2D game up as a small 3D
  scene with a moving sun, shadows, glow and miniature focus. Heights are
  learned as you play and saved per game; the game and your controls stay 2D.
- **Sharper Super FX 3D.** Super FX polygons are redrawn on the GPU at 2 to
  4 times the resolution, and Super FX, CPU and SA-1 overclocks are governed
  so a game never runs faster than it should.
- **Cheats.** Game Genie, Pro Action Replay and raw codes in **Quick Menu ›
  Cheats**, saved per game and recorded in save states.
- **RetroAchievements.** Sign in under **Quick Menu › Achievements**. Unlocks
  count as softcore for now: RetroAchievements accepts hardcore unlocks only
  from emulators it has approved.
- **Rebuild lab.** Per game, RetroForge maps the code your game runs into
  functions you can watch, name and hook from Lua, and checks every frame
  against the plain emulator until the game is proven. Built on your machine
  from your ROM; no game code ships.
- **More you can switch on:** run-ahead, smoother motion on 120 Hz screens,
  and shader presets you can name and save.
- **A calmer Quick Menu.** Esc closes only the top layer, Cheats and
  Achievements have their own pages, settings that go online sit together
  under **Settings › Online**, and the library opens on a Resume card.

## Install

Download the archive for your computer, unpack it and run `retroforge`
(`retroforge.exe` on Windows), keeping the `profiles` folder next to it.
The builds are not signed: on macOS allow the app once under System
Settings › Privacy & Security; on Windows choose "More info", then "Run
anyway". Linux needs a Vulkan or OpenGL driver and ALSA for sound. See the
[player guide](https://github.com/bpmforge/retroforge/blob/v0.10.0/docs/site/src/getting-started.md).
